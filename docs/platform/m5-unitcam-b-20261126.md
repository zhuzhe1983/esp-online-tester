# Unit Camera U109-B: independent enclosed camera controller

U109-B is a complete **ESP32-WROOM-32E / 4 MB Flash / OV2640 65° camera controller**, supplied with its enclosure and mounting clip. It is not an empty enclosure, U109 bare board, the U109-X two-lens DIY kit or TimerCAM. Its independent catalog/knowledge/model and factory-reference entry keep `runtime.board: null`, `status: unavailable`; the workbench and mini retain **尚未完成适配** with Run hidden. PSRAM is unspecified and stays unknown.

The exact [U109-B document](https://docs.m5stack.com/en/unit/Unit_Camera) and [storefront](https://shop.m5stack.com/products/unit-cam-wi-fi-camera-with-enclosure-ov2640) establish the identity, UART RX G16/TX G17, blue LED G4 and camera pin map. The official comparison table distinguishes all three U109 variants. Sources, final URLs, original acquisition times, bytes and hashes are retained in [source-receipts.json](evidence/m5-unitcam-b-20261126/source-receipts.json).

The reviewed M5_Hardware fixed tree contains no exact U109-B CAD, matching footprint or embedded STEP; this product page provides no such structure link. Its preview therefore uses **its own photos and dimension drawing**, with the normal-lens DIY approximation's primitive construction explicitly reused as a starting method. No donor hardware, firmware identity or PCB layout is inherited. The target drawing corrects the independent model:

The assembled solid envelope measures approximately **24 × 40.0000001 × 11.0000000 mm**. This verifies consistency with the selected drawing constraints, not manufacturing accuracy or an independent photograph overlay. Hidden wall/clip details, lens optical shape, connector depth, PCB layout and contact diameters remain estimates. The provided Lego clip is documented but omitted from the camera's nominal envelope and unverified assembly position. The Grove mouth is open, and attached cables remain disabled because physical pin-1/mating orientation is uncalibrated.

The rear has two distinct paper regions and an exposed WROOM metal shield. Paper pixels exclude the shield and both terminal windows; the shield's own photograph is mapped only to its metal carrier. Paper has a 0.025 mm display gap; the shield decal uses the actual shield face. This offset prevents z-fighting and is not a measured adhesive thickness or manufacturing tolerance.
