# Stamp-S3 PIN1.27 model refresh

The exact PIN1.27 cover STL and all 28 nonduplicate shared manufacturer STEP solids retain their source coordinates, with only the documented -1.3 mm assembly Z translation. The alternate STEP cover is omitted. The official PIN1.27 page directly links both sources.

The exact official socket footprint references 17-pin and 11-pin KiCad 1.27 mm headers at X ±7.62 mm. Their original standard geometry is retained and partitioned into black plastic and metal without removing triangles. The footprint is a socket fixture: its Z offsets are not used as standalone product measurements. Header orientation and seating against the PCB underside follow exact-SKU product photographs. Receiving sockets are not part of this model. The KiCad license, credits, original STEP files and exact M5Stack footprint are retained as hashed rebuild inputs. They are not relabeled as M5Stack manufacturing CAD.

The native black board underside carries the rectified and masked official PIN1.27 photo. Native holes and border clip the projection; raised headers, orange clips and brass post region are excluded from the texture. A photo-informed annular lip supplements the simplified source post, with unmeasured thickness disclosed. The screw retains its source geometry plus a separate hex appearance mark. UNKNOWN remains on the inward board face; the declared cover/screw/control group moves +10 mm for inspection, leaving PCB, connectors, headers and post stationary. This viewing arrangement does not establish a physical teardown procedure.

The default photographed orange cover has no applied paper sticker. The supplied loose sticker is documented but not placed on an unsupported surface. Exact-SKU component photos are 800×800; the 1200×1200 packaging image does not add board detail. Oblique front/back comparisons are qualitative, with IoU 0.789819/0.732323 retained rather than labeled a dimensional pass. The texture source is also the backside appearance reference, so that comparison is not independent electronic-layout validation. Simplified USB shape, source copper/plating and missing electronic heights remain visible limitations.

Validation used the committed public rebuild command, headless Chromium, the public inspection button, original/gold/purple materials, and source-photo overlays. Four declared objects move exactly +10 mm and return exactly; non-shell material states are unchanged. Public reset and repeated toggling restore assembly, and UNKNOWN is readable with all parts present. The model is still partial. Firmware behavior belongs to separate runtime evidence.
